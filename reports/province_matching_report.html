What is TAT's 78th value?

77 of the 78 distinct ATT_PROVINCE_ID values follow the "10XX" pattern and were matched to a real province (75 via the mode of each id's records' postal-code prefix, cross-validated against the standard geocode; the remaining 2 -- 1005 and 1045 -- had no usable postcode anywhere in their records and were hand-identified by searching their attraction names, resolving to Kamphaeng Phet and Yasothon respectively). The 78th value is the literal string "1" (13 records). Its record names are junk/placeholder data -- "test", "test 13120844", "Hello world!" (three times) -- not a real 78th province. It reads as an unset/default province_id sentinel in the source system, a data-quality defect rather than a geography, and is excluded from the crosswalk.

What are flood's 2 missing provinces?

thailand_flood_risk_areas.csv has rows for 75 of the 77 provinces. Diffing its (prefix-stripped) province list against the master shows the 2 entirely absent provinces are Phuket (ภูเก็ต) and Ranong (ระนอง) -- there is not a single row, for any tambon, in either province, across all 17 years the file covers. This isn't a spelling mismatch (every other province matches exactly once its "จ." prefix is stripped; Bangkok is the one province stored without that prefix) -- the source simply has no flood-history data for these two provinces. Documented as a coverage gap in the source, not corrected: any dashboard metric derived from this file should show these two provinces as "no data," not "zero risk."
